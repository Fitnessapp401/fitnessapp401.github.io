Workout app for beginners over 35

Get stronger, one step at a time.

Destination Health gives you a simple training plan built around your week, and a coach that adjusts it after every workout. Made for people who are new to training, short on time, or getting back into it after a break.

Download on the App Store

Free on iPhone. No account needed.

Who it is for

Destination Health is made for adults over 35 who want to feel fitter and stronger but don't know where to start, and don't have hours to spend at the gym.

New to training

No experience needed. Every exercise has a picture and three plain tips, and the plan starts gently.

Short on time

Pick how many days a week and how long you have. Sessions fit your week, from about 15 minutes.

Coming back after a break

The plan builds you up step by step and respects old injuries, so you can start again without overdoing it.

How it works

1. Tell us about you

Your goals, how many days you can train, how long you have, and whether you train at home or at the gym. Mention anything that hurts and the plan avoids it.

2. Follow today's session

Open the app and today's session is ready. Follow along one move at a time, with a timer that beeps so you don't have to watch the screen.

3. Your coach adjusts

After each workout you say how it felt. Too easy? It gets a bit harder. Too hard? Easier exercises or fewer sets. Something hurt? That move gets swapped.

A look inside

What you get

  • A training plan built around your goals, your week and your time
  • Home workouts with no equipment, home workouts with dumbbells or bands, or gym workouts
  • Pictures and three simple tips for every exercise
  • Swap any exercise for an easier or harder version, and undo it in one tap
  • An easy walk or a short stretch on rest days
  • A workout history and a short check-in every few weeks to see your progress
  • Reminders on your training days, at a time you choose
  • No account. Your name and workout history stay on your phone. AI coaching is optional.

Good to know

Destination Health is free to download on iPhone. It gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice — if you have a health condition, check with your doctor before you start.
