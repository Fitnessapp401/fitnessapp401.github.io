Strength training at home for beginners

You don't need a gym to get stronger. A few simple exercises, done regularly, make a real difference â€” even with no equipment at all.

Five beginner exercises you can do at home

Chair stands

Stand up from a chair and sit back down slowly. Strong legs for everyday life.

Incline push-ups

Hands on a table or bench, body straight. Easier than floor push-ups and great for the chest and arms.

Glute bridges

Lie on your back, feet flat, and lift your hips. Kind to the lower back and good for the glutes.

Bird dog

On all fours, reach one arm forward and the opposite leg back. Balance and a stronger core.

Plank

Hold a straight line on your forearms. Start on your knees if you need to.

How often and how much

Two or three sessions a week is plenty to start. Begin with one or two sets of each exercise, stopping a couple of repetitions before it gets really hard. When a session starts to feel easy, add a set, a few repetitions, or a slightly harder version of the exercise.

Common beginner mistakes

  • Doing too much in the first week and then stopping.
  • Skipping the warm-up.
  • Pushing through sharp pain â€” stop and swap the exercise instead.
  • Changing the plan every day. Repeating the same few exercises is how you improve.
