Quick help

How do I change my plan?

Open Your plan on the progress tab to see your weeks. To change just today's session, tap Change on Today's session.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Go to Settings and use Training reminders. You can also change the time there.

How do I turn AI coaching on or off?

Go to Settings and use Adjust my plan with AI. When it is off, the app uses simple built-in rules and sends nothing.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings and tap Reset all progress, or remove the app. Your data is stored on your phone, so this deletes it.

The timer sounds are too loud or I don't want them

Go to Settings and turn off Timer sounds.
